How points are earned
Points measure your contribution to DEXLESS during each season.
1Earn during the week

Activity from Monday to Sunday (UTC) is counted for that week.

2Points land every Monday

Weekly points are distributed Monday 00:00 UTC and added to your season total.

3Season snapshot

When a season ends, totals and ranks are locked. A new season starts from zero.

FAQ
Do points carry over between seasons?

No. Each season starts from zero. Past seasons stay visible from the season menu above.

How are points earned?

Trading volume, referrals, vault deposits and Rewards missions all add points. Trading is the main source.

What are tiers?

Tiers show how far you are in the current season: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond.

Why did I get 0 points this week?

Points only come from activity in that week. If you didn't trade, refer or deposit, the week shows 0.
